About Me

Curious About Data. Focused on Insights.

I'm Rania Hashem, a Data Analyst with a genuine curiosity for what numbers can reveal. I enjoy working through the full analytics process: collecting and cleaning data, running exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand what's really going on, and turning the findings into clear, useful business intelligence.

I'm particularly interested in identifying trends, understanding business performance, and uncovering the "why" behind the metrics. Whether it's tracking KPIs, comparing segments, or spotting opportunities for improvement, I like to ask the right questions before reaching for the tools.

Strong data visualization is at the heart of my work. I believe an insight only creates value when people can understand it, so I focus on presenting findings through clean charts, interactive dashboards and straightforward storytelling that decision-makers can act on.
